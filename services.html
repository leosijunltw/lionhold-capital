Our services

Structured advisory across financial and technology services.

We combine local market understanding, international experience and rigorous analysis to help clients evaluate opportunities, manage risk and execute with confidence.

Financial services

Investment, private client advisory, audit and credit expertise within one integrated offering.

Financial services · 01

Investment services

01

Investment management

Supporting Cambodian individuals and companies in identifying, evaluating and managing investments across real estate, local development, business acquisitions and corporate restructuring.

02

Investing into Cambodia

Supporting international individuals and companies seeking to invest in Cambodia. We help clients understand the local market, identify and assess suitable opportunities, conduct commercial due diligence, evaluate risks and execute the investment.

03

Deal advisory and investment matching

Connecting credible businesses and investment opportunities with suitably aligned investors, while assessing transaction structures, commercial viability, expected returns and material risks to support informed execution.

Financial services · 02

Private client advisory

01

Succession planning

Helping founders and family-owned businesses plan the transfer of ownership and leadership. We assess the options, value the business, and structure a transition that protects continuity for the company, the family and the people who depend on it.

02

Business sale advisory

Guiding owners through the sale of their business, from preparing credible financial information and assessing value to identifying suitable buyers and supporting due diligence, negotiation and completion.

03

Company formation in Cambodia

Helping Cambodian and international founders establish a company in Cambodia from start to finish. We advise on the most suitable structure, coordinate incorporation and the registrations and licences the business requires, and support the practical set-up, from a corporate bank account to accounting and tax foundations, so the company is ready to operate.

Our private client advisory complements, and does not replace, the legal and tax advice of appropriately licensed professionals. Where such advice is needed, we work alongside the client’s advisers.

Financial services · 03

Audit services

01

Contract verification

Confirming the authenticity, legal existence and principal terms of contracts, while checking that supporting information is consistent with the proposed transaction.

02

Asset and title verification

Verifying the existence, ownership and stated characteristics of land and other assets, including their conformity with available titles, records and agreed specifications.

03

Financial review and due diligence

Reviewing financial information, supporting records and key assumptions to identify inconsistencies, material risks and areas requiring further investigation.

Our audit services focus on commercial review, verification and due diligence. They do not constitute a statutory financial audit unless expressly agreed and undertaken through appropriately licensed professionals.
