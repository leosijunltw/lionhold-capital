Sovichea holds a Master’s in Finance from the University of Cambridge, having been awarded the prestigious WiF Scholarship. Previous experience spans investment banking at Terafi Capital, audit at Grant Thornton and capital markets at Lloyds Banking Group.

At Lloyds, Sovichea developed extensive experience in capital markets and applied data analytics through SQL, Power BI, Tableau and advanced Microsoft Excel—bringing a rigorous combination of financial analysis, transaction experience and data-led decision-making to Lionhold Capital.

Sochet holds a Master’s in Statistics from the University of Cambridge, where he received a $65,000 academic scholarship, and a Bachelor’s in Economics from the London School of Economics and Political Science, graduating one year early following high exam performance.
