Careers at Lionhold

Build at the intersection of finance and technology.

We actively recruit both full-time analysts and interns who want to solve meaningful commercial problems while developing across disciplines.

01 · Full-time

Analyst opportunities

We actively recruit full-time analysts with strong foundations in finance, technology or advisory work.

Requirements

  • Proficient in both English and Khmer
  • Graduated from a top Cambodian or international university
  • At least one year of experience in accounting, investment, data science, data engineering, AI or management consulting

How to apply

Email us with your CV and a link to your LinkedIn profile.

02 · Internships

Two, three or six months

Depending on availability, we offer two-, three- and six-month internships, with the opportunity to receive a full-time analyst offer by the end of the internship.

Requirements

  • Proficient in both English and Khmer
  • Currently studying for, or graduated with, a Bachelor’s degree from a top Cambodian or international university

How to apply

Email us with any one of the following:

  • A link to your LinkedIn profile
  • Your CV
  • A short description of where you studied and any previous experience

Previous work experience is optional. There is no minimum prior-experience requirement for internships.
